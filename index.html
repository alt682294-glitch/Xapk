📁 XAPK

APKPure bundle format. A ZIP containing base.apk, optional split APKs, and OBB expansion data. We extract the base APK and package everything for you.

🔐 APKM

APKMirror bundle format. Contains split APKs. DRM-free files are extracted directly. Encrypted APKMs require the official APKMirror Installer.
